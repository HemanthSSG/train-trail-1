Built With Railway & Geospatial Data

Where applicable: OpenStreetMap, OpenRailwayMap, Indian railway datasets, railway timetable APIs and verified train running-status APIs.

Map data, station data, schedule data, live train data and live gate data are kept distinct. RAILCUE does not claim official Indian Railways affiliation unless such authorization exists.

Railway Safety Comes First

RAILCUE provides journey awareness and railway infrastructure information. Always follow railway signals, barriers, traffic signs and instructions from railway authorities.

Do not use RAILCUE as confirmation that a railway crossing is safe to cross.
